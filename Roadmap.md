## Roadmap

1. Stand up YaCy in private-peer isolation, routed through the I2P HTTP proxy, serving jaysden.i2p (see `yacy-setup.md`)
2. Confirm which registries, trackers and the DHT are reachable, from a running router
3. Seed union and old address-book retries
4. Clearnet and Irc2P address mining
5. Link crawler with per-site rate limits
6. Torrent DHT and tracker ingestion (confirm SAM 3.3 support on the chosen router first)
7. Submission form and ping endpoint
8. Text-only snapshot archive and the removal pipeline
9. Hard-rule filter, then the classifier
10. Staged model filtering for ambiguous cases
11. Origin classification, native versus derivative tagging
12. Published coverage report
13. Statistics page, the 50/50 native versus derivatives split (see `eepsite stat webpage format.md`)
14. A maintained hosts.txt feed to subscribe to
